Whether you are starting to use Atomic CRM, or want to migrate from another CRM, you can import your contacts, companies, tasks, notes, tags and deals in a few clicks. You can also export your contacts and companies in CSV format, and your contacts as vCard files.

## Import Data From CSV

Atomic CRM imports contacts, companies and deals from CSV files. Two buttons open the same import dialog:

- **Import data**, on the initial user onboarding page, lets you choose the resource to import into.
- **Import CSV**, on the contacts, companies and deals pages, imports into that resource directly.

<video src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a1133cf0-0287-4fc0-b61b-5a0b88617cfc" controls className="w-full aspect-1304/814"></video>

For each resource, the dialog offers a sample CSV file to download, showing the expected columns. Columns the CRM does not know are ignored, and columns you omit are left empty.

Every resource accepts a `sales_email` column, naming the consultant / trainer in charge of the imported record by their CRM email address. An empty cell — or an address nobody on the team uses — leaves the record to whoever runs the import.

### Contacts

```csv
first_name,last_name,gender,title,background,first_seen,last_seen,has_newsletter,status,tags,linkedin_url,company,email_work,email_home,email_other,phone_work,phone_home,phone_other,sales_email
John,Doe,male,Sales Executive,,2024-07-01T00:00:00+00:00,2024-07-01T11:54:49.95+00:00,false,in-contract,"influencer, developer",https://www.linkedin.com/in/johndoe,Acme,john@doe.example,john.doe@gmail.com,jdoe@caramail.com,659-980-2015,740.645.3807,(446) 758-2122,marie@vitalo.fr
Jane,Doe,female,Designer,,2024-07-01T00:00:00+00:00,2024-07-01T11:54:49.95+00:00,false,in-contract,"UI, design",https://www.linkedin.com/in/janedoe,Acme,,,jane@doe.example,659-980-2020,740.647.3802,,
```

When importing contacts, companies and tags will be automatically matched if they exist on the system, or imported otherwise.

### Companies

```csv
name,sector,size,linkedin_url,website,phone_number,address,zipcode,city,state_abbr,country,description,revenue,tax_identifier,sales_email
Acme,Information Technology,50,https://www.linkedin.com/company/acme,https://acme.example,555-0100,1 Main Street,10001,New York,NY,USA,Long-time partner on print projects,5M,US-123456789,marie@vitalo.fr
Globex,Industrials,250,,https://globex.example,555-0111,42 Industrial Road,60601,Chicago,IL,USA,,20M,,
```

The `name` column is required. `sector` is matched against the company sectors configured in your CRM, either by stored value or by the label displayed in the app; a sector matching none of them is left empty.

### Deals

```csv
name,company,category,stage,amount,expected_closing_date,description,sales_email
New website,Acme,Website design,Opportunity,12000,2026-09-30,Redesign of the public website,marie@vitalo.fr
Print campaign,Globex,Print project,Proposal Sent,4500,2026-10-15,,
```

The `name` column is required. The `company` column holds a company name: an existing company is reused, an unknown one is created. `category` and `stage` are matched against the deal categories and stages configured in your CRM, by stored value or displayed label. Since a deal always belongs to a stage, an empty or unrecognized `stage` falls back to the first stage of your pipeline.

## Export Contacts To CSV

In the contacts and companies pages, an export button allows to download the list of contacts or companies in CSV format.
